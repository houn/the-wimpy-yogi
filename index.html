About the practice

Jenly picked up teaching a few months ago, and The Wimpy Yogi is where her Mindful Yoga practice is taking shape: a small, steady space based in Mandaue City, Cebu.

She teaches the way she'd want to be taught — no assumption that you already know what a pose is supposed to feel like, no rush toward anything you're not ready for. Beginners are met exactly where they are, one class at a time.

If you're new to the mat, the Sunday and Monday morning classes are the easiest place to start — chances are you'll recognize the nerves.

Balance isn't about not wobbling. It's about what you do the moment you do.

— Jenly, The Wimpy Yogi

From Jenly

Rooted before you rise

Every beginner asks some version of the same question in their first tree pose: what do I do with the wobble? I ask myself something similar most days I teach — and the answer's the same either way. Nothing dramatic. You find the floor again through your standing foot, soften your gaze, and let the sway settle instead of fighting it.

That's most of what this practice is, on both sides of the mat. Not holding still, but noticing what pulls you off balance and returning to it, again and again, without making it a big deal.

If you want to work on this together, Sunday and Monday mornings are Beginners Yoga — come as you are, wobble included.
